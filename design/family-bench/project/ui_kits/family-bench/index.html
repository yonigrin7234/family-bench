Design principles

Four commitments the system enforces.

Every component, color choice, and piece of copy ladders up to these. When we break one, we replace it with something just as deliberate — never by accident.

01

Modern court

Official enough to hold up in front of a judge. Soft enough to survive daily use at 11 pm. Near-black ink, 0.5px hairlines, almost no shadow.

02

Sans is the workhorse

Inter carries the interface. Serif italic is a guest — used for emotional moments and legal formality. Never both at once.

03

Honest about what's hard

The voice is a calm advisor, not a hype-bot. If something is slow or unlikely to work, the app says so. No false reassurance, no "you've got this."

04

One step, not a dashboard

Home shows the single most important next action. Everything else is a tap away. The app reduces decisions; it doesn't spray them.

The kit in use

Mobile captures. Desktop commands.

The mobile app is the capture surface — one-handed, pocket-size, crisis-ready. The desktop web is the war room — case map, evidence lenses, filing wizard. Same tokens, different densities.
