"Your case, kept carefully."
The seal is a soft oxblood circle with an italic serif "F" — court-official but not bureaucratic. The wordmark pairs Family (ink) with Bench (oxblood) in Instrument Serif. No separator; the color carries the emphasis.
Use the full lockup on launch and marketing. Use the seal-only in-app: navigation, favicon, file-signed badges, chain-of-custody certificates. Never recolor. Never put the seal on a gradient.
