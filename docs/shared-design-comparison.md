## Decisions and acceptance boundaries

Both onboarding sets remain references; the implemented optional Welcome does not turn eight mockups into a compulsory sequence. Briefcase now supplies explicit selection/retrieval paths and Trust Center describes current capabilities. Verify the real hearing/offline and failure-recovery workflows, reconcile remaining consent/first-run choices, and validate operational controls before making stronger guarantees. See [connected-workflow checks](connected-workflows-2026-09-05.md) and [requirements coverage](requirements-coverage.md) for implemented scope and remaining subfeatures.

The shared canvas's legal, safety, zero-access, blockchain, sealing and verification language is **design content, not evidence of a working guarantee**. Existing encryption, hashes, private storage and revision receipts must be described precisely; they do not imply court acceptance, emergency protection, zero-access architecture, blockchain anchoring or public verification. The initial official-form workflow has its own template, field and preparation tests; full form coverage, e-filing and service still require further implementation and validation. Prototype sample cases, dates, totals and provider states must never become real-user data or live status.

Record the expanded source before a detailed fidelity pass; then apply it to the phased workflow in the UI/UX plan. Validate the account → case → capture/original → review → factual export path first, including errors and recovery. This design comparison adds reference scope to the backlog; it does not replace the 79-section specification, claim every prior conversation is captured, or change the [deployment and verification status](production-foundation-status.md).
